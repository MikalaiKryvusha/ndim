## 3. Обещание, отличия, доверие

**Обещание (под первым экраном)**

> You give stars to what you know and love — films, books, series, games, the things you do —
> from zero to ten. Those stars become your description, and from there the NDim Space takes over.
> It compares your description with everyone else's and checks every new arrival against you,
> **while you get on with your life**.
>
> You never have to go looking. The Space looks for you, and shows you what it found.

**Четыре отличия**

> **1 · No ads, no one else's trackers**
> There is not a single advert and not a single third-party tracker on this page or inside the
> Space. We count four numbers a day — how many people came, how many moved the stars here, how
> many came in as guests, how many created an account — and those numbers hold no name, no email,
> no address and no device.

> **2 · There is nothing to buy**
> NDim Space is free for everyone, everywhere, with no purchases and no subscriptions. Every
> feature is the same for everyone, and a higher place in someone's list **is not for sale**.

> **3 · The same in both directions**
> Similarity is symmetrical: however similar you are to someone, they are exactly that similar to
> you — one number for the two of you. Your gender does not enter the calculation, and neither
> does how long you have been here. Only what you described, and the stars you gave it.

> **4 · Objective mathematics only**
> **Similarity is proximity multiplied by commonality.** Proximity is how alike you and another
> person rated the same things. Commonality is how widely your two descriptions overlap.
> NDim Space **does not use the subjectivity of the human eye, nor the unpredictability of
> artificial intelligence systems**.

**Доверие**

> Leaving takes **one click**: the deletion page is always open, and we erase your data before we
> erase the account itself — and if something did not delete, we will tell you plainly what is
> left. No support emails, no talking you out of it.
> Nobody sees your ratings but you. Others see only how close you are — never what that closeness
> is made of.

**Подпись к интерактиву**

> Give stars to five things, and the neighbours move closer or further away: the distances on the
> map are real. There are five here so you can see how it works; the NDim Space has 5,111. The
> same core does the counting as inside. The people on the map are system characters — they live
> in the Space, and they are marked as such honestly.

## 4. Четыре вопроса

### В1. Кнопка входа — какая из трёх?

Русская — «Найти людей по душе». Дословного английского у неё нет, поэтому три варианта, и они
звучат по-разному:

- **А) (рекомендую) `Find your people`** — самое живое и короткое; в современном английском это
  устойчивый тёплый оборот со смыслом «свои люди, твои по духу». Обещает награду, а не работу.
- **Б) `Find kindred spirits`** — ближе всего к «по душе» буквально, но звучит книжно и слегка
  старомодно; часть аудитории прочтёт это как поэзию, а не как кнопку.
- **В) `Find people like you`** — самое буквальное и самое нейтральное; повторяет подзаголовок,
  зато не оставляет ни малейшего разночтения.
